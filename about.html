About this Ranking

A little background on me, this project and how the ranking was created.

Why I created this

I've been playing video games for as long as I can remember. Over the years, many games have left a lasting impression on me. Some because of their gameplay, others because of their stories, soundtrack or the memories I associate with them.

This website is my personal collection and ranking of the games that mean the most to me.

How the ranking works

The ranking is based on my personal experience and a combination of different factors:

  • Fun factor
  • Gameplay
  • Story and characters
  • Atmosphere and music
  • Lasting impression
  • Replayability
  • Competitiveness

It's completely subjective. It's all about the games that I enjoyed the most.

Gaming is the entertainment of the future, the present and the past. This is mine.

— SilasDV
