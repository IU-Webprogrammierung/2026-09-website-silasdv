Here you will find my game ranking, sorted by my personal preference.

The first three entries are available below. More games will be added as I build this website.

My Ranking

  1. Celeste

    Platform: PC

    Playtime: about 400 hours

    Rating: 10/10

    Release year: 2018

    Genre: Platform

    A breathtaking platformer that explores the topic of depression. With its mods and large modding community, this game is a timeless joy.

  2. Hollow Knight: Silksong

    Platform: PC

    Playtime: about 100 hours

    Rating: 10/10

    Release year: 2025

    Genre: Platform / Adventure

    If Hollow Knight left you wanting more, Silksong will not disappoint. A sequel that takes the best parts of the first game and turns them up a notch. Probably the best 20 euros I have ever spent on a game.

  3. Custom Robo Arena

    Platform: Nintendo DS

    Playtime: about 45 hours

    Rating: 9.9/10

    Release year: 2006

    Genre: Fighting / RPG

    Robot battles? Robot battles! A game that sadly never received a sequel. Probably the best Nintendo DS game ever made.
